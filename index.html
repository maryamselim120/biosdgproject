Energy Management Protocol Specifications

Normal Active Scanning

Full operational power draw (100%). Gradient amplifiers, cold-head chillers, tube pre-heating, and workstation displays operate at capacity for instant scan readiness.

Low Power Mode (Soft Standby) (-30%)

During 15-30 min idle gaps. Powers down user consoles, puts gradient power supplies in low-quiescent state while preserving tube temp & cryo-refrigeration. Warm-up time: < 60 sec.

Deep Sleep (-60%)

Activated overnight & weekends. Throttles auxiliary HVAC, puts CT rotor drivers and PET detectors in ultra-low standby, maintaining only essential helium compressor safety loops. Warm-up time: 4-6 min.
